Freight Pulse · Issue 33

Shanghai–New York jumps 10% while Asia–Europe keeps sliding

The divergence that would define September started here. A double-digit week on the US East Coast against an 8% fall into the Mediterranean.

SeaFreightPrices Market Desk · 18 August 2026 · 3 min read

Index levels assessed 13 August 2026. This is the weekly briefing as it was published — it is not updated afterwards. For the current position on your lanes, use the rate explorer.

What moved, and why

  • The composite rose 1% to $4,339 per 40ft, held back by Asia–Europe even as the transpacific ran hard.
  • Shanghai–New York gained 10% and Shanghai–Los Angeles 6%.
  • Asia–Europe fell for another week, Shanghai–Genoa down 8% and Shanghai–Rotterdam down 5%.
  • Carriers were restricting space aggressively: ten sailings cancelled in each of the previous two weeks, with seven more planned for the following week.

What it meant for a quote

If you quoted both major corridors off one composite this week you were wrong in both directions — by 10% on the US East Coast and 8% into the Mediterranean. This is the week the year's divergence became impossible to average away, and the point at which lane-level benchmarks stopped being optional.
