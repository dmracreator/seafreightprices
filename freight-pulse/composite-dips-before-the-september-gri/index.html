Composite dips as transpacific cools into the September GRI

A quiet week before a loud one. The index gave back a little, blanking eased, and the 1 September increase lands today across seven carriers.

SeaFreightPrices Market Desk · 1 September 2026 · 3 min read

Index levels assessed 27 August 2026. This is the weekly briefing as it was published — it is not updated afterwards. For the current position on your lanes, use the rate explorer.

What moved, and why

  • The composite eased 1% to $4,473 per 40ft, with both transpacific and Asia–Europe softer.
  • Shanghai–New York gave back 2% to $9,333 and Shanghai–Los Angeles was unchanged at $6,818, after several weeks of hard gains.
  • Blank sailings for the following week dropped to four from seven, meaning capacity was coming back rather than tightening.
  • CMA CGM, COSCO, Evergreen, Hapag-Lloyd, HMM, Yang Ming and ZIM all filed for a general rate increase effective today on the East Asia to USA lane.
  • Drewry expected a less volatile week ahead, with demand resilient and carriers still managing supply.

What it meant for a quote

A week where capacity was returning and rates were easing, immediately before seven carriers pushed an increase. That combination rarely holds in full. The useful question the following week was not whether the increase was announced but what proportion of it actually reached invoices.
